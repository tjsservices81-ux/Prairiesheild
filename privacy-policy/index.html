Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, how this website handles your information. It is a general statement, not legal advice. It must be reviewed and approved by the business before launch.

What we collect

When you submit an estimate request on this site we collect the information you enter: your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, the service you are interested in, any message you include, and any offer you have saved.

Why we collect it

We use this information for one purpose: to respond to your request. That includes contacting you to schedule an inspection, answer questions and provide your free estimate.

Saved offers

If you save a promotional offer, it is stored in your own browser using local storage. Nothing is sent to us until you submit a form, at which point the offer name is included so we can apply it to your estimate. You can clear it by clearing your browser data.

Analytics and spam protection

This site may use basic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you. Our forms are protected by Google reCAPTCHA, which is subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms of service.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not share your form submissions with third parties except as needed to operate this website (for example our form-handling and hosting provider) or as required by law.
