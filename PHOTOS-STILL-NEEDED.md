### The habit that closes this

You clearly have plenty of "before" photos — twelve of your uploads were
damage shots with no matching "after". They cannot be used: a real "before"
beside a placeholder "after" reads as a fake comparison.

On every job from now on, before anyone touches anything and before scaffold
goes up:

1. Take the **before**. Stand somewhere you can find again — mark it.
2. When the job is done, stand in the **same spot**, same height, same
   framing, and take the **after**.
3. Send the two together and say which service it was.

Keep the before photo open on your phone when you take the after and line it
up against it. That is the whole technique.

Do not zoom in on the after, or shoot it in bright sun when the before was
in shade — it looks like a trick even when it is not.

At one pair per service, this is roughly **one job per service**, then it is
done.
