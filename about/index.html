Who We Are

Built on Trades That Belong Together

Most contractors pick a lane: waterproofing, foundations or masonry. In Chicago, that split causes real problems. A stair-step crack through brickwork is often a foundation issue. A chimney pulling away from a house is usually a footing that has settled. A wet basement can start with a failed mortar joint two storeys up.

Prairie Shield covers all three, so when we inspect your property we can follow the problem to its actual source instead of stopping at the edge of our specialty.

We work across Chicago and the six surrounding counties on bungalows, two-flats, greystones, condo buildings and commercial properties — waterproofing, underpinning, repointing, rebuilding and everything between.
