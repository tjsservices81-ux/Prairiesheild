5 · Options

Review and edit what that sheet says

Assistance programs open and close constantly — Chicago's own Home Repair Program is not taking new registrations in 2026. So this sheet deliberately points people at the phone numbers and offices that route them to whatever is running now, rather than naming programs that may already be closed. Check it before you send it.
